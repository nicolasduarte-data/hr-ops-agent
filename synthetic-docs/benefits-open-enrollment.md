# Benefits Open Enrollment — Meridian HR Guide

**System**: Workday HCM (Benefits)
**Applies to**: All benefits-eligible employees
**Enrollment window**: Annual — typically October 15 – November 1
**Last updated**: 2026-01-10
**Owner**: HR Operations / Total Rewards

---

## Overview

Meridian's annual Benefits Open Enrollment (OE) runs through Workday. During the OE window, eligible employees can:
- Enroll in, change, or waive medical, dental, and vision coverage
- Change FSA/HSA contribution elections
- Update life insurance beneficiaries
- Add or remove dependents

**Elections made during OE take effect January 1 of the following plan year.** Outside of OE, changes are only permitted following a qualifying life event (QLE — see below).

---

## Accessing the Benefits Enrollment Worklet

1. Log into Workday at `workday.meridian-hr.internal`.
2. On the Home screen, locate the **Benefits** worklet. During the OE window, an **Open Enrollment** task also appears in your Workday **inbox** — this is the fastest entry point.
3. Click **Open Enrollment** → review the enrollment overview page. Workday displays your current elections alongside available changes.

**Note:** If you do not see the Open Enrollment task in your inbox during the OE window, check your eligibility status with HR Operations. Employees on LOA during OE should contact HR — a special process applies.

---

## Making Changes

### Medical / Dental / Vision
1. From the Open Enrollment page, click **Change** next to the benefit type.
2. Select your plan from the dropdown. Coverage tiers available: Employee Only, Employee + Spouse/DP, Employee + Children, Family.
3. If adding a dependent, you will be prompted to add dependent details (name, date of birth, relationship, SSN for dependent verification). This data is stored in Workday.
4. Review the premium costs shown — Workday displays the per-pay-period employee contribution for each plan.
5. Click **Confirm and Continue**.

### FSA / HSA Elections
1. Select **Health Care FSA** or **Health Savings Account** (HSA — available only with HDHP plan selection).
2. Enter your annual election amount. Workday calculates the per-period deduction automatically.
3. IRS contribution limits apply and are enforced by Workday. If you enter an amount above the limit, Workday will block the submission.

### Life Insurance Beneficiaries
1. Navigate to **Life Insurance** in the OE worklet.
2. Click **Edit Beneficiaries**. Add or update primary and contingent beneficiaries.
3. Beneficiary designations do not require annual review but should be updated after major life events.

## Qualifying Life Events (Outside OE)

If you have a qualifying life event (marriage, divorce, birth/adoption of a child, loss of other coverage), you have **30 days from the event date** to make changes in Workday.

To initiate a QLE:
1. In Workday, navigate to **Benefits → Change Benefits Reason**.
2. Select the qualifying event type and enter the event date.
3. Workday routes the request to HR Operations for verification. You may be asked to provide documentation (e.g., marriage certificate, birth certificate).

Outside the 30-day window, changes cannot be made until the next OE period.

---

## Confirmation and Evidence of Coverage

After completing enrollment:
1. Workday generates a **Benefits Statement** — download it from the Enrollment Confirmation screen. Keep a copy for your records.
2. Insurance ID cards are mailed within 2–3 weeks of the effective date by the carrier. Digital cards are available via the carrier's app/portal.
3. If you do not receive an ID card, contact the carrier directly. HR Operations maintains carrier contact lists at the ServiceNow HR Knowledge Base.

## Common Issues

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Open Enrollment task not in inbox | Confirm eligibility with HR Ops; check if on LOA |
| Can't add dependent — error on SSN field | Use Workday's "No SSN" toggle if dependent SSN is not yet available; document and update within 90 days |
| HSA option not showing | HSA requires HDHP selection — select the HDHP plan first |
| OE elections not showing post-January | Allow 1–2 pay cycles for payroll deductions to begin; check with Payroll Ops if not corrected by February |
