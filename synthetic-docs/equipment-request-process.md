# Equipment Request Process — Meridian IT & HR Guide

**System**: ServiceNow HR Service Delivery (HRSD) + IT
**Applies to**: All employees
**Last updated**: 2026-01-12
**Owner**: IT Operations / HR Operations

---

## Overview

Hardware and peripheral equipment requests at Meridian are submitted through ServiceNow. Standard new-hire equipment is provisioned automatically during the onboarding workflow — this guide covers requests outside of that process: replacement equipment, additional peripherals, ergonomic accommodations, and equipment for role changes.

---

## Request Types and Routing

| Request type | ServiceNow catalog item | Fulfilling team |
|---|---|---|
| Replacement laptop/computer | IT Equipment — Replacement Request | IT Operations |
| Additional monitor or peripheral | IT Equipment — Peripheral Request | IT Operations |
| Ergonomic accommodation (chair, desk, keyboard) | HR — Ergonomic Accommodation Request | HR Operations + Facilities |
| Equipment for new role / promotion | IT Equipment — Role Change Equipment | IT Operations (manager-approved) |
| Home office stipend reimbursement | HR — Home Office Stipend | HR Operations / Finance |

---

## Submitting an Equipment Request

1. Navigate to the ServiceNow portal at `servicenow.meridian-hr.internal`.
2. Click **Catalog** → search for the applicable catalog item (see table above) or browse **IT Services** or **HR Services**.
3. Complete the request form:
   - **Requestor** — pre-populated with your name
   - **Equipment type / model preference** — select from approved models list; custom requests require manager approval
   - **Business justification** — required for all non-standard requests
   - **Delivery location** — office floor/desk or home address (home delivery available for remote employees)
4. Submit. A **Request Item (RITM)** is generated. You will receive a confirmation email with the RITM number for tracking.

## Approval Workflow

- **Standard requests** (replacement within the approved list): auto-approved after manager confirmation.
- **Non-standard or high-value requests** (>$500): require manager approval + IT Operations sign-off.
- **Ergonomic accommodations**: require HR Operations review; medical accommodation requests (ADA-related) are handled separately by Employee Relations.

You will receive approval status notifications via ServiceNow and email.

## Fulfillment SLAs

| Request type | Target fulfillment |
|---|---|
| Standard replacement (in-stock) | 3 business days |
| Standard new peripheral (in-stock) | 3 business days |
| Non-standard / ordered item | 7–14 business days |
| Ergonomic accommodation | 5 business days (standard items) |

---

## Returning Equipment

Equipment must be returned when you:
- Leave Meridian (offboarding checklist triggers a return RITM automatically)
- Receive a replacement unit (old unit returned to IT via the same RITM process)
- Change roles and the equipment standard changes

IT Operations will provide a prepaid shipping label for remote employees. Office employees return equipment to the IT Help Desk on Floor 2.

**Failure to return equipment within 14 days of the return request triggers an asset escalation** — HR may deduct replacement cost from final paycheck per local law, in accordance with your employment agreement.

## Common Issues

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| RITM not showing in my ServiceNow requests | Check the email confirmation for the RITM number; search by RITM in ServiceNow portal |
| Delivery address not saving correctly | Enter address in RITM comments if the form field rejects it |
| Ergonomic request pending >5 business days | Ping HR Ops directly at `hr-ops@meridian-hr.internal` with your RITM number |
| Received wrong equipment | Comment on the RITM and assign back to IT Operations with a description of the discrepancy |
