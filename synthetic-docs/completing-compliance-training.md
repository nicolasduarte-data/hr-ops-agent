# Completing Compliance Training — Meridian HR Guide

**System**: SAP SuccessFactors Learning (LMS)
**Applies to**: All employees
**Mandatory completion window**: January – March (annual cycle)
**Last updated**: 2026-01-05
**Owner**: HR Compliance / HRIS Team

---

## Overview

Meridian's mandatory compliance training is delivered through the SAP SuccessFactors Learning Management System (LMS). All employees must complete their assigned compliance curriculum each calendar year. Completion is tracked in SF LMS and reported to Legal and HR Compliance. Incomplete training after the deadline may result in system access suspension pending completion.

---

## Accessing Your Training Assignments

1. Log into SF EC at `successfactors.meridian-hr.internal`.
2. Click **Learning** (top navigation) or the **My Learning** tile on the Home screen.
3. Your assigned curricula appear under **Required Training**. The panel shows:
   - **Course name**
   - **Due date**
   - **Status** (Not Started / In Progress / Completed)
   - **Completion percentage** (for multi-module courses)

If you do not see training assignments and you are past your onboarding date, contact `hris-support@meridian-hr.internal` — your LMS enrollment may not have been triggered.

---

## Completing a Training Module

1. Click the course title from your **Required Training** list.
2. Click **Start** (or **Continue** if previously started).
3. Training modules launch in a new browser tab. Supported browsers: Chrome 110+, Edge 110+, Firefox 110+, Safari 16+. **Internet Explorer is not supported.**
4. Complete all sections. Most modules include a final assessment — passing score is ≥80% unless otherwise specified.
5. After passing, the course status in LMS updates to **Completed** within 15 minutes. If it does not update after 30 minutes, refresh the Learning page; if still not updated, raise an ITSM incident assigned to the HRIS team.

## Annual Compliance Curriculum

Meridian's standard annual curriculum (assigned to all employees):

| Course | Duration | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Code of Conduct & Ethics | 45 min | Includes annual attestation |
| Data Privacy & GDPR Basics | 30 min | Required for all employees (global) |
| Information Security Awareness | 30 min | Includes phishing simulation confirmation |
| Anti-Harassment & Respectful Workplace | 60 min | Manager version is longer (90 min) |
| Conflict of Interest Disclosure | 20 min | Annual attestation embedded |

Additional role-specific modules may be assigned by your HRBP or manager (e.g., Export Controls for certain roles, SOX Awareness for Finance team members).

## Completion Certificates

For courses that generate a completion certificate:
1. From the course detail page (after completion), click **View Certificate**.
2. Download as PDF. Certificates include your name, course name, completion date, and a unique certificate ID.

Certificates are stored in your SF LMS transcript indefinitely. Access: **Learning → My Transcript**.

---

## Manager View — Team Completion Status

Managers can view their team's training status:
1. Navigate to **Learning → Team Learning**.
2. Select the team member or filter by assignment type.
3. Export the view to CSV for compliance reporting (Finance and Legal may request this).

---

## Late Completion and Escalation

If an employee has not completed required training by the published deadline:
1. HR Compliance sends an automated reminder at 14 days before deadline (via SF EC email notification).
2. At the deadline, HR Compliance notifies the employee's manager.
3. 14 days post-deadline: HR Compliance may request IT to suspend non-critical system access until training is completed.

Employees on LOA during the training window receive a 30-day extension upon return.

## Common Issues

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Course not launching (blank screen) | Check browser compatibility; disable browser extensions; try incognito mode |
| Assessment passed but status not updating | Wait 30 min; if still pending, raise ITSM incident to HRIS |
| Don't see any training assigned | Contact hris-support — LMS assignment may not have triggered |
| Need a completion certificate for external use | Download from My Transcript; contact HR Compliance if you need an official letter on company letterhead |
| Course assigned twice | Duplicate assignment — raise ITSM to HRIS to remove the duplicate |
