# Annual Performance Review — Meridian HR Guide

**System**: SAP SuccessFactors Performance & Goals (PMGM)
**Applies to**: All regular employees and their managers
**Cycle**: January 1 – December 31, review period Q1 following year
**Last updated**: 2026-01-08
**Owner**: HR Business Partners / HRIS Team

---

## Overview

Meridian's annual performance review cycle runs in SF SuccessFactors Performance & Goals (PMGM). The process has four stages: goal setting, mid-year check-in, self-evaluation, and manager rating. All stages are completed in the PMGM module — do not use email or offline forms.

---

## Review Cycle Timeline

| Stage | Typical dates | Who acts |
|---|---|---|
| Goal setting in PMGM | February – March | Employee + Manager |
| Mid-year check-in | June – July | Employee + Manager |

## Stage 1 — Goal Setting

1. Log into SF EC at `successfactors.meridian-hr.internal`.
2. Navigate to **Performance** (top nav) → **Goals**.
3. Click **Create Goal**. Enter:
   - **Goal name** — clear, action-oriented
   - **Category** — Business Goal, Development Goal, or Compliance Goal
   - **Metric / target** — measurable where possible
   - **Due date** — use December 31 for annual goals
   - **Weight** — HR recommends total weights sum to 100%
4. Click **Save**. Goals cascade from your manager if your organization uses cascading goals — you will see them in a separate panel.
5. Once goals are finalized, click **Send to Manager** to share for alignment. Your manager can comment but goals are employee-owned.

---

## Stage 2 — Mid-Year Check-In

The mid-year check-in is a brief status update, not a formal rating.

1. Navigate to **Performance → Performance Reviews → [Your Review Form]**.
2. The mid-year form has a progress field for each goal (On Track / At Risk / Off Track) and a freetext comment section.
3. Complete your section and click **Submit to Manager**. Your manager adds comments and returns it.
4. No formal rating is assigned at mid-year. The check-in is informational.

---

## Stage 3 — Year-End Self-Evaluation

1. Navigate to **Performance → Performance Reviews → [Your Review Form]** when the self-evaluation window opens (notification sent via SF EC email to your work address).
2. Complete all sections:
   - **Goal ratings**: rate your own performance on each goal (1–5 scale, per Meridian's rating guidance)
   - **Competency ratings**: rate yourself on the three standard Meridian competencies (Collaboration, Execution, Growth Mindset)
   - **Overall summary**: 200–500 word free text, first-person, past-tense
3. Review the **Rating Guideline** link in the form header — 5-point scale definitions are embedded.
4. Click **Submit to Manager**. Your self-evaluation is locked from further edits after submission.

---

## Stage 4 — Manager Rating and Calibration

Managers access direct reports' forms via **Team Performance** in the PMGM nav. After entering preliminary ratings, calibration sessions are facilitated by HR Business Partners. Final ratings are entered post-calibration. Employees cannot see manager ratings until the communication window opens.

---

## Viewing Your Rating

Once the communication window opens:
1. Navigate to **Performance → Performance Reviews → [Your Review Form]**.
2. Your manager's ratings and comments are visible in the **Manager Section** tab.
3. You will receive a task in your SF EC inbox to acknowledge the review. Click **Acknowledge** after your review discussion with your manager. Acknowledgement does not indicate agreement — it confirms receipt.

## Common Issues

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Review form not showing in Performance nav | Form may not be launched yet — check cycle dates with your HRBP |
| Self-evaluation field grayed out | Window may be closed; contact HR Ops — late submissions require HRBP override |
| Goal weights don't sum to 100% | PMGM allows non-100% but shows a warning; HR recommends resolving before submission |
| Rating visible but shows "—" | Calibration not complete — ratings publish after HR unlocks the communication window |
