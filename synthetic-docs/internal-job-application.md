# Internal Job Application — Meridian HR Guide

**System**: SAP SuccessFactors Recruiting (RCM)
**Applies to**: All regular employees (minimum 6 months in current role)
**Last updated**: 2026-02-01
**Owner**: Talent Acquisition / HR Operations

---

## Overview

Internal job openings at Meridian are posted in SAP SuccessFactors Recruiting (RCM). Eligible employees can apply directly through the internal career site. Internal applicants receive priority review — recruiters are required to respond within 10 business days of application.

**Eligibility requirement**: You must have been in your current role for at least 6 months before applying for another internal position. Exceptions require your current manager's written approval and HR Business Partner sign-off.

---

## Finding Internal Job Postings

1. Log into SF EC at `successfactors.meridian-hr.internal`.
2. Navigate to **Careers** (top navigation or the Home tile).
3. Filter by **Internal** in the Employment Type dropdown to see only internal openings.
4. You can further filter by: Location, Department, Job Family, and Full-Time/Part-Time.
5. Click any job title to view the full job description, requirements, hiring manager name, and application deadline.

---

## Applying for an Internal Position

1. On the job posting page, click **Apply**.
2. Your SF EC profile pre-populates the application — review your personal information, work history, and skills before submitting.
3. Upload or select your resume. You can use the resume on file in SF EC or upload a new version. Internal applications do not require a cover letter unless specified in the posting.
4. Complete the application questionnaire (if present). Questions are specific to the role.
5. Agree to the Meridian Internal Application Terms and click **Submit Application**.

**Important:** Applying for an internal role does not notify your current manager. However, if you advance to the interview stage, you will be asked to notify your manager before a reference check is conducted.

---

## Application Status

Track your application in SF EC:
1. **Careers → My Applications** — shows all active and past applications with current status.

Status values:
- **Submitted** — application received, pending recruiter review
- **Under Review** — recruiter is actively reviewing
- **Interview Scheduled** — next steps have been initiated
- **Offer** — verbal or written offer extended
- **Not Selected** — position filled or your application was not progressed

Internal applicants are always notified of the outcome, including if not selected.

---

## Interview Process for Internal Applicants

The interview process for internal candidates mirrors the external process:
1. Recruiter phone screen (~30 min)
2. Hiring manager interview (1–2 rounds)
3. For senior roles: panel interview with stakeholders

**Manager notification**: Before a reference check is initiated, you must notify your current manager. HR will send you a task in your SF EC inbox prompting this step. If you have concerns about confidentiality, contact your HR Business Partner.

---

## After an Offer is Accepted

If you accept an internal offer:
1. HR initiates a **Position Change** in SF EC, effective on your transfer date.
2. Your manager is notified by HR Ops — your current manager works with Talent Acquisition on a transition timeline (standard notice is 2–4 weeks unless otherwise agreed).
3. Your new job code, department, and cost center update in EC on the effective date. Downstream systems (Workday, Okta, etc.) update via the standard replication schedule.
4. Benefits and PTO accruals continue without interruption. There is no rehire gap.

## Common Issues

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Can't see the Careers tile in EC | Check with HRIS — the tile may need enabling in your user account |
| Eligibility error when applying (< 6 months in role) | Request exception approval from your manager + HRBP before applying |
| Application status not updating | Allow 24 hours; if still stale, contact Talent Acquisition |
| Position is listed as "External Only" | Some roles are not open to internal transfer per business need; check with your HRBP |
