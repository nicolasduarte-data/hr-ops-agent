# Accessing Pay Statements — Meridian HR Guide

**System**: SAP SuccessFactors Employee Central Payroll (ECP)
**Applies to**: All employees on the SF ECP payroll
**Last updated**: 2026-01-22
**Owner**: Payroll Operations

---

## Overview

Pay statements (pay stubs) for Meridian employees on the SF Employee Central Payroll (ECP) are available in the **Employee Self-Service (ESS)** portal embedded in SF EC. Pay statements are published within 24 hours of payroll completion each pay period.

**Note for Workday subsidiary employees**: If you joined Meridian via the acquired subsidiary and have not yet been migrated to ECP, your pay statements remain in Workday under **Pay → Pay Slips**. Contact Payroll Ops if you are unsure which system applies to you.

---

## Accessing Your Pay Statement

1. Log into SF EC at `successfactors.meridian-hr.internal`.
2. Navigate to **Pay** (top navigation) → **Pay Statements**.
3. Select the pay period from the dropdown. Meridian runs a **bi-weekly payroll** — pay dates are every other Friday.
4. Click **View** to display the pay statement in your browser, or **Download PDF** to save a copy.

Alternatively, use the SF EC global search: type "Pay Statement" → click the first result.

---

## Understanding Your Pay Statement

A Meridian pay statement has three main sections:

### Earnings
- **Regular** — base pay for the period
- **Overtime** (if applicable) — hours × OT rate
- **Bonus / Commission** — shown separately; paid on the bonus pay date, not the regular pay date
- **Holiday Pay** — for company holidays falling within the pay period

### Deductions
- **Federal / State / Local Income Tax** — withheld per your W-4 elections
- **Social Security / Medicare** (FICA)
- **Benefits** — medical, dental, vision, FSA/HSA per your enrollment (bi-weekly amounts)
- **Retirement** — 401(k) employee contribution + employer match (if applicable)

### Net Pay
Deposited via ACH to your bank account on the pay date. Allow until end of business on Friday if the deposit is not showing by morning — ACH timing varies by bank.

## Updating Direct Deposit Information

To change your bank account for direct deposit:

1. In SF EC: **My Profile → Pay → Payment Information**.
2. Click **Edit** and enter your new routing number and account number.
3. Changes are effective the **next full pay cycle after the submission date**. If you change your account mid-cycle, the current payroll run uses the previous account.
4. Paper checks are not available — all employees must maintain a valid bank account on file.

---

## Year-End Tax Documents (W-2 / ADP / Local)

W-2 forms are published in SF EC by January 31 each year.

1. Navigate to **Pay → Tax Documents**.
2. Select the tax year from the dropdown.
3. Download PDF. You may consent to electronic-only delivery in **Profile → Preferences → Tax Document Delivery** — if opted out, a paper copy is also mailed to your address on file in EC.

**If your address changed in Q4 and you didn't update EC before December 31**, a paper W-2 may be mailed to the old address. Contact Payroll Ops immediately: `payroll-ops@meridian-hr.internal`.

## Common Issues

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Pay statement not available after pay date | Allow until end of day Friday; if not posted by Monday, contact Payroll Ops |
| Net pay different from expected | Review deductions section; benefits elections may have changed at OE or after a QLE |
| Pay statement shows wrong tax state | Tax withholding is based on your work location in EC — confirm your EC location is correct |
| W-2 not showing in Tax Documents | Allow until January 31; after that date contact Payroll Ops |
| New bank account not used for current paycheck | Changes take effect next full pay cycle — current payroll already ran |
